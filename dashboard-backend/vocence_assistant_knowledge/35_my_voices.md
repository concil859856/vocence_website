# My Voices

My Voices is your library of saved custom voices designed in Voice Design. Open it at vocence.ai/studio/my-voices. From here you can preview each saved voice, generate new speech from any of them, and delete voices you no longer want. Voices are private to your account — no other Vocence user can see them or generate from them.

# What each voice card shows

Each saved voice appears as a card with the voice's name, the date it was saved, an inline audio player to preview the original sample, a generate-new-speech action, a download link for the original sample, and a delete button. Click the play button to hear the original A/B sample you saved; click generate to render new text in that voice for 25 credits.

# Generating from a saved voice

Pick the voice from My Voices, type the new text (up to 300 characters, same limit as TTS), and click generate. Cost is 25 credits per generation, same as a regular TTS request. Output is WAV, plays in the result panel, and is saved to History. The voice is deterministic — every generation from the same saved voice uses the same locked-in design instruction, so subsequent generations are stylistically consistent.

# Deleting a voice

Click the trash icon on the card to delete. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone — the design instruction and the original A/B sample are removed. Past generations you made with that voice stay in History and remain playable; deleting the voice doesn't break old History entries.

# My Voices limits

Normal-plan users can save up to 5 custom voices. Premium-plan users have no cap — they can save as many as they want. Hitting the cap on Normal means you'd need to delete a voice first or upgrade to Premium for unlimited slots. Voice Design A/B previews still work even if you're at the cap; you just can't save the result until you free a slot.

# Voices are not transferable

Saved voices live on your account only. There's no current way to share a saved voice with another user, export it as a portable file, or use it on another account. If you want a voice that's reproducible across accounts, write down the design description and recreate it with Voice Design on each account — but expect minor variation between recreations.
