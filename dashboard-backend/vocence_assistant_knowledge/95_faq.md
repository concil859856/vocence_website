# FAQ — do I need a credit card to start?

No. New accounts get 300 free credits at signup, no card required. You can generate audio immediately. You only need to pay if you want more credits or want Premium features (unlimited custom voices, no history expiry, Developer API access).

# FAQ — why didn't my generation succeed?

Generations can fail for several reasons: the input was rejected by the safety filter, the network had a transient capacity issue, or the model returned an error. If a generation fails on Vocence's side, the credits are auto-refunded to your balance. If credits aren't refunded within a few minutes after a clearly-failed attempt, email space@vocence.ai with the timestamp and we'll resolve it.

# FAQ — why does the public Playbook I made not appear in browse?

Public Playbooks may be moderated. If yours doesn't appear, it may be under review or may have been removed for an Acceptable Use violation. Check your email for any notice from us, and if you think the removal was a mistake, email space@vocence.ai.

# FAQ — can I clone a celebrity's voice?

No. Cloning real people without explicit, documented consent is forbidden. Public figures and celebrities are not exempt. Use Voice Design to create an original voice in any style you like, or use one of the 28 sample voices that ship with Studio.

# FAQ — does Vocence work in non-English languages?

The TTS and Voice Cloning models on Subnet 78 are predominantly English-trained today. Some non-English support exists via the underlying models but is not the focus of Q1 evaluation. Multi-language coverage is part of the roadmap. Check vocence.ai/docs for the latest supported-language list.

# FAQ — how do I become a miner or validator?

The open-source repo at github.com/vocence-78/vocence has Miner Setup and Validator Setup guides, mirrored at vocence.ai/docs/miner and vocence.ai/docs/validator. Miners need a Bittensor wallet, a Hugging Face account, a Chutes deployment, and at least 12GB of GPU. Validators need Chutes access (granted by the Vocence team), the owner API endpoint, Hippius bucket credentials, and an OpenAI key.

# FAQ — can I use Vocence audio commercially?

Yes. Audio you generate is yours to use, including for commercial projects, subject to the Acceptable Use rules — most importantly, no impersonation of real people without consent and no copyrighted source material. Enterprise contracts are available via vocence.ai/sales for teams that need custom commercial terms or higher quotas.
