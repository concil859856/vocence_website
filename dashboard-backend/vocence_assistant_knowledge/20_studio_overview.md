# Studio overview

Studio at vocence.ai/studio is the single workspace where every generation happens. The left sidebar lists every feature: Voice Design, Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text, Voice Cloning, Text-to-Music, My Voices, Agents, Playbooks, History, and Voice Chat (coming soon). Each generation costs credits, deducted from your balance. Failed generations are auto-refunded.

# Studio sidebar items

- Voice Design — describe a voice in plain English and get an A/B preview to save (120 credits per preview).
- Text-to-Speech — turn text up to 300 characters into spoken audio with a chosen style (25 credits per generation).
- Speech-to-Text — transcribe an uploaded or recorded audio clip into text (20 credits per transcription).
- Voice Cloning — reproduce a reference voice saying new target text up to 2,000 characters (50 credits per clone).
- Text-to-Music — generate a track from a prompt, with Fast/Balanced/Max quality modes (50 credits per generation).
- My Voices — your saved custom voices from Voice Design (25 credits per generation from a saved voice).
- Agents — your own voice-chat agents with custom system prompts, voices, and knowledge bases.
- Playbooks — personal or public collections of audio tracks.
- History — every generation you've ever made on the account, retained 7 days on Normal or indefinitely on Premium.
- Voice Chat — real-time free-form voice conversation, coming soon. Logos, the floating Vocence Assistant, is a precursor that's available now.

# Cross-cutting Studio behavior

Your credit balance is always visible in the top-right of authenticated pages and on each generation card. Generations that exceed your balance are blocked with an "insufficient credits" error before being submitted; nothing is charged in that case. Generations that fail mid-flight (capacity, model error, internal timeout) are auto-refunded — you only ever pay for successful renders. While a generation is processing, you'll see a "Generating…" pill on the submit button and a queue position if your request is waiting on a busy worker pool.
