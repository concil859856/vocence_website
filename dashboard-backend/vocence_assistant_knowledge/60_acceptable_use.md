# Acceptable Use — what's allowed

By using Vocence you agree to the Terms of Service at vocence.ai/terms. The full list of restrictions and the moderation rules for public Playbooks live there. The most important rules in plain language: don't break the law, don't generate harmful content, don't impersonate real people without consent, don't upload copyrighted material you don't have rights to, don't use public Playbooks for spam, and don't harass other users.

# Forbidden content categories

Vocence does not allow content that is hateful, violent, sexually explicit, threatening, harassing, discriminatory, related to self-harm, related to terrorism, or that involves minors in any sexualized or harmful context. This applies to TTS output, voice cloning, music lyrics, voice-design descriptions, and public Playbook metadata. Public Playbooks are the most visible surface and get the most moderation attention.

# Voice cloning consent

You may only clone voices for which you have permission — your own voice, voices you have explicit, documented consent to use, or voices clearly licensed for synthesis. Cloning a real person, including public figures and celebrities, without documented consent is forbidden. This is the most common reason accounts get suspended.

# Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive public content to space@vocence.ai. Vocence may review, restrict, or remove public content at any time without prior notice. Repeat or severe violations can lead to account suspension or termination. Generated audio you keep private is not actively reviewed, but the rules still apply if it's later shared.
