# Playbooks

Playbooks are personal or public collections of audio tracks — think of them as playlists you can share. Open them at vocence.ai/studio/playbooks. Each Playbook has a title, a cover image, a list of tracks in a chosen order, and a visibility setting (private or public).

# Creating a Playbook

Click "New Playbook" on the Playbooks page. The new Playbook starts as an Untitled draft — click the title to rename it inline. Add a cover image by clicking the 132×132 cover slot and either dragging a file in or picking one from your computer (PNG and JPEG both work). Add tracks by importing from your Music History, or upload your own audio.

# Reordering tracks

Drag the handle icon next to any track to drop it into a new position. The order is saved automatically — the next time you (or anyone, if it's public) opens the Playbook, the tracks play in the order you set. Reordering is owner-only; viewers of a public Playbook see the order but can't change it.

# Public vs private

Toggle a Playbook between private and public from the visibility control. Switching to public requires a confirmation step, since public Playbooks are visible to all users on the platform. Private Playbooks are visible only to you. Switching back to private hides it again immediately. The toggle can be flipped any number of times.

# Public Playbook page

Public Playbooks show the title, cover image, track count, and the owner's user avatar and name. Anyone on Vocence can play tracks from a public Playbook without signing in beyond their own session. There is no like/comment system today — public visibility is a "share to all" surface, not a social feed.

# Public moderation

Public Playbooks must comply with the Terms of Service and Acceptable Use rules. Vocence may review, restrict, or remove public content at any time without prior notice. Repeat or severe violations can lead to account suspension or termination. Report abusive public content to space@vocence.ai. The moderation focus is highest on public Playbooks because they're the most visible surface — private ones are not actively reviewed.

# Cover image

The cover image slot is 132×132 pixels. Both PNG and JPEG are accepted. The image is shown as the Playbook's thumbnail in lists, on the public browse page, and on the Playbook's own page. If you don't upload one, a default placeholder is shown.

# Where Music tracks go

When you generate music in Studio, the result is saved to History automatically and made available for adding to any Playbook. Music tracks can live in multiple Playbooks at once — adding a track to one Playbook doesn't move it out of any other.
