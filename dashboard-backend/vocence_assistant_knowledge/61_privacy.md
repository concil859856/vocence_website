# Privacy

Vocence's full privacy policy is at vocence.ai/privacy. Highlights: account data we store is your email, name, and profile picture (from Google), plus credits, plan, and transaction history. Audio you submit is processed to fulfill your request and stored in your account so you can play it back from History or Playbooks. Vocence does not sell personal information. Cookies are used for sign-in sessions and basic analytics.

# Data rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. To exercise any of those rights, contact space@vocence.ai. Account deletion removes your account, history entries, and saved voices on request — the deletion can take a few days to propagate fully.

# Service providers

Vocence uses third-party service providers for hosting, payment processing (Stripe, NOWPayments), and analytics. These providers may process limited data on Vocence's behalf, under their own privacy policies and Vocence's data-processing agreements with them. The full list and details are in the privacy policy at vocence.ai/privacy.
