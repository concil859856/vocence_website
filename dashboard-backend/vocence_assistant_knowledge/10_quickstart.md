# Quick start — sign in and your first generation

Open vocence.ai, click Sign In, continue with Google. New accounts get 300 free credits automatically — no credit card required. Once signed in, click Studio and pick a feature from the left sidebar. Type your prompt or upload your audio, hit generate, and your result appears in a player you can listen to, download, or save. That's the whole flow.

# Sign-in

Vocence uses Google as the sole identity provider — there is no email/password signup. New accounts receive a one-time welcome bonus of 300 credits on first sign-in. You can sign out anytime from the navigation menu. Your profile, plan, credit balance, payment history, and Developer API keys all live at vocence.ai/account.
