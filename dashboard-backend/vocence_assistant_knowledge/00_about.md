# What Vocence is

Vocence is a decentralized voice AI platform at vocence.ai. Users sign in with Google, get free credits, and use Studio to generate audio — text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice cloning, custom voice design, and music generation. There is no separate setup; the whole thing runs in the browser.

# What powers Vocence

Under the hood Vocence is Bittensor Subnet 78 — an open, permissionless network where independent miners deploy voice AI models and validators score them on quality. Rewards go to the models that perform best on objective metrics. End users don't have to think about any of that — Studio just routes their request to the best available model behind the scenes.

# Two sides of Vocence

There are two sides of Vocence that often confuse people. The "product side" is vocence.ai — Studio, Account, Pricing, Docs, Blog. That's what most users interact with. The "subnet side" is the open Bittensor network at github.com/vocence-78/vocence — that's where miners and validators participate. The product side consumes the subnet; the subnet exists independently of the product.
