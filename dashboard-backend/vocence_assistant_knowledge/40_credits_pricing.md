# Credits — the single billing unit

Every Studio generation consumes credits. Credits are a single, simple unit — there is no separate metering for different features. The per-action prices are fixed: Text-to-Speech 25, Speech-to-Text 20, Voice Cloning 50, Voice Design A/B preview 120, generating from a saved "My Voice" 25, Text-to-Music (any mode) 50. New accounts get 300 free credits at signup.

# Refunds for failed generations

If a generation fails on Vocence's side — e.g. temporary network capacity, model error, internal timeout — the credits are automatically returned to your balance. You only ever pay for successful generations. Refunds happen automatically; you don't need to email support unless something looks stuck.

# Where to buy credits and see pricing

Buy credits at vocence.ai/pricing. The page shows three packs (Normal, Premium, Enterprise) in both card and crypto pricing. Crypto packs include bonus credits versus the card-equivalent price. After payment confirms, credits are added to your account automatically and a transaction row appears under vocence.ai/account.
