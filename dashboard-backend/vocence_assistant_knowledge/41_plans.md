# Normal plan

Normal is Vocence's entry pack: $12 by card buys 4,000 credits, or $20 by crypto buys 7,000 credits (the crypto pack includes bonus credits as a thank-you for paying on-chain). Normal users get access to all Studio features (TTS, STT, Voice Cloning, Music, Voice Design), can save up to 5 custom voices, and have their generation history retained for 7 days. New accounts also get 300 free credits at signup, on top of any pack they buy.

# Premium plan

Premium is $24 by card for 10,000 credits, or $40 by crypto for 16,000 credits. Premium includes everything in Normal plus three things: history never expires, unlimited custom voices in My Voices, and access to the Developer API (TTS, STT, Voice Cloning, Music). Premium is required for API access — the API tab on /account is locked until a successful Premium purchase clears.

# Enterprise plan

Enterprise is custom-priced and contracted via vocence.ai/sales. It's aimed at teams, apps, and larger-scale deployment. Enterprise customers get full API support for product and platform integration, dedicated onboarding, commercial support, private quotas, and operational flexibility. Pricing is determined per engagement.

# All packs are one-time

Vocence doesn't currently run subscriptions — all credit packs are one-time top-ups. You buy credits, you use them at your own pace. Your plan tier (Normal / Premium / Enterprise) is determined by your most recent qualifying purchase. Premium status persists across packs once you've bought it.
