# Payment methods

Vocence accepts payment in two ways. Credit/debit cards run through Stripe — currently the card option is in "coming soon" mode in the UI; check vocence.ai/pricing for live status. Crypto payments run through NOWPayments and are live now. Multiple coins and networks are supported, including BTC, ETH, USDT, and USDC.

# How crypto payments work

After picking a crypto pack you're redirected to a NOWPayments invoice. The invoice stays denominated in USD; NOWPayments shows the exact crypto amount due based on real-time rates. Once the on-chain payment confirms, credits are added to your account automatically and a transaction row appears under vocence.ai/account. Crypto packs include bonus credits versus the card-equivalent price.

# Where to manage payments

All payment history, invoices, and transaction records live under vocence.ai/account. If a payment is delayed or the credits don't show up after on-chain confirmation, email space@vocence.ai with the transaction ID and we'll resolve it.
