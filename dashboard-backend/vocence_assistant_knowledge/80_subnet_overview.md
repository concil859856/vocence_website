# Vocence Subnet 78 — what it is

Vocence is Bittensor Subnet 78 — a decentralized voice intelligence subnet focused on building and evaluating AI voice models. It registered on the Bittensor network in April 2026. The subnet is an open, incentivized marketplace where miners compete by deploying voice AI models and validators evaluate their quality, with rewards distributed based on measurable performance.

# Subnet current focus

The subnet's Q1 focus is Prompt-based Text-to-Speech (PromptTTS) — miners deploy models that generate speech from text plus a natural-language voice instruction (e.g. "A calm middle-aged male voice with a warm tone, speaking slowly"). Future phases will expand to Speech-to-Text (STT), Speech-to-Speech (STS), voice cloning, and other multimodal voice tasks.

# Why a subnet, not a centralized service

Voice model quality improves fastest when many independent teams experiment with architectures, datasets, and training strategies. Bittensor enables that decentralized experimentation without centralized gatekeepers. Miners earn TAO proportional to the real performance of the models they deploy — overfitting and prompt cheating are penalized to maintain quality. Validators compete on evaluation accuracy, and Vocence's evaluation pipeline is fully public and reproducible.
